# Smithies Studio 👋

**Bespoke software, built around the way your business actually works.**

We're Jonathan and Amanda Smithies, a husband-and-wife studio in Chepstow, UK. We build and look after custom, browser-based software for growing businesses and for founders with an idea that needs building.

Most of the businesses we work with didn't set out to need software. They grew, and the spreadsheets, manual steps and tools that almost fit started to cost more time than they saved. Others arrive with an idea and nothing off the shelf that can carry it. Either way, we start with how things really work today and build the thing that fits.

---

## 🚀 What we build

- Custom web applications
- Booking and scheduling systems
- Customer portals and admin dashboards
- Internal business systems and process automation
- Connecting the systems you already pay for
- Improving or taking over software you already have
- Proof-of-concept builds, to test an idea before the full build
- Brochure websites for local businesses

We also work as a technical delivery partner for agencies and studios who need extra capability without stretching their own team.

## 🧭 How we work

1. **Listen.** A free, no-obligation conversation about how your business runs now.
2. **Plan.** We agree what to build, what it costs and roughly how long it will take.
3. **Build.** In clear stages, so you see it take shape and can steer it.
4. **Support.** We stay on to maintain, fix and improve it.

Most projects are fixed price to an agreed scope. You own your data, and you own your application once it's paid for.

## 👥 Who's here

- **Jonathan Smithies**, Partner & Co-Founder. The software engineer behind everything we ship, with 10+ years of turning awkward business problems into software.
- **Amanda Smithies**, Partner & Co-Founder. Looks after everything around the build: communication, project management, contracts, design and how the studio runs.
